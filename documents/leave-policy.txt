TechVista Solutions — Leave Policy

Paid Time Off (PTO)

TechVista Solutions provides generous paid time off to support work-life balance. PTO accrual is based on years of service:

- 0 to 2 years of service: 15 days per year (1.25 days per month)
- 3 to 4 years of service: 20 days per year (1.67 days per month)
- 5 or more years of service: 25 days per year (2.08 days per month)

PTO begins accruing from the first day of employment. New employees may use accrued PTO after completing their 90-day probationary period.

PTO Carryover Policy

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the following year or they will be forfeited. Unused PTO beyond the 5-day carryover limit is not paid out at year-end.

PTO Request Procedure

All PTO requests must be submitted through our HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for requests of 3 or more consecutive days. Single-day requests require at least 48 hours notice. Manager approval is required for all PTO requests. During peak business periods (quarterly close, product launches), PTO may be limited and requires additional advance notice.

Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 sick days per year, separate from PTO. Sick leave can be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or caring for an immediate family member. A doctor's note is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days. Unused sick days do not carry over and are not paid out.

Parental Leave

TechVista supports new parents with the following leave benefits:

- Primary caregiver: 16 weeks of paid leave at 100% salary
- Secondary caregiver: 6 weeks of paid leave at 100% salary
- Adoption and foster care: Same benefits as biological parents
- Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption date
- Employees may take parental leave in one continuous block or split it into two blocks with manager approval

Bereavement Leave

Employees are entitled to paid bereavement leave as follows:

- Immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 5 days
- Extended family (grandparent, in-law, aunt, uncle): 3 days
- Close friend or colleague: 1 day

Additional unpaid leave may be requested and will be considered on a case-by-case basis.

Jury Duty

Employees called for jury duty will receive their full salary for up to 10 business days. If jury duty extends beyond 10 days, the company will pay the difference between jury duty compensation and regular salary for up to 30 total days. Employees must provide a copy of the jury summons to HR within 3 business days of receiving it.

Company Holidays

TechVista observes the following paid holidays:

1. New Year's Day — January 1
2. Martin Luther King Jr. Day — Third Monday of January
3. Presidents' Day — Third Monday of February
4. Memorial Day — Last Monday of May
5. Independence Day — July 4
6. Labor Day — First Monday of September
7. Thanksgiving Day — Fourth Thursday of November
8. Day after Thanksgiving — Fourth Friday of November
9. Christmas Eve — December 24
10. Christmas Day — December 25
11. New Year's Eve — December 31

When a holiday falls on a weekend, the preceding Friday or following Monday will be observed.

Unpaid Leave of Absence

Employees who have completed at least 1 year of service may request an unpaid leave of absence for up to 30 days for personal reasons. Requests must be submitted in writing to HR at least 30 days in advance. Benefits coverage may continue during unpaid leave, but the employee is responsible for paying the full premium cost.

Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)

Eligible employees (those who have worked at least 12 months and 1,250 hours) are entitled to up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per year under FMLA. Qualifying reasons include serious personal health conditions, caring for a family member with a serious health condition, or bonding with a new child.
