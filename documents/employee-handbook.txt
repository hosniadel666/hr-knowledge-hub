TechVista Solutions — Employee Handbook

Welcome to TechVista Solutions

Welcome to TechVista Solutions! We are a technology company dedicated to building innovative software solutions that empower businesses worldwide. This handbook outlines the policies and expectations that guide our workplace culture.

Our Mission

To deliver cutting-edge technology solutions that transform how businesses operate, while fostering a culture of innovation, collaboration, and continuous learning.

Our Core Values

1. Innovation First: We encourage creative thinking and experimentation. Every team member is empowered to propose new ideas and challenge the status quo.
2. Integrity: We conduct our business with honesty, transparency, and ethical standards.
3. Collaboration: We believe the best solutions come from diverse teams working together.
4. Customer Focus: Our customers' success is our success. We strive to exceed expectations in every interaction.
5. Continuous Learning: We invest in our people's growth through training, mentorship, and development opportunities.

Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to maintain professional behavior at all times. This includes treating colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and dignity. Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind will not be tolerated. Violations should be reported to HR immediately and will be investigated thoroughly.

Work Hours and Flexibility

Standard work hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. However, TechVista embraces flexible scheduling. Employees may adjust their start time between 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM with manager approval, provided they complete 8 hours per day and are available during core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

Remote Work Policy

TechVista operates a hybrid work model. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements may be approved on a case-by-case basis by department heads. Remote employees must maintain a reliable internet connection, be available during core hours, and attend in-person team meetings as scheduled (typically once per month).

Dress Code

TechVista maintains a business casual dress code for office days. Jeans and sneakers are acceptable. For client-facing meetings, business professional attire is expected. On Fridays, casual dress is encouraged.

Communication Expectations

We use Slack as our primary communication tool for day-to-day messaging. Email is reserved for formal communications and external correspondence. Employees are expected to respond to messages within 4 business hours during working hours. For urgent matters, direct phone calls are appropriate.

Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted quarterly. Each review includes a self-assessment, peer feedback, and a one-on-one discussion with your manager. Reviews evaluate performance against goals set at the beginning of each quarter, alignment with company values, and professional development progress.

Promotion Criteria

Promotions at TechVista are based on demonstrated impact, leadership qualities, and readiness for increased responsibility. Employees are eligible for promotion consideration after a minimum of 12 months in their current role. Promotions are reviewed during our biannual talent review cycle in January and July.

Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential company information, including trade secrets, client data, financial information, and proprietary technology. All employees sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) upon hire. Breaches of confidentiality may result in immediate termination and legal action.

Expense Policy

Business expenses must be pre-approved by your manager for amounts exceeding $100. Submit expense reports within 30 days of incurring the expense through our expense management system. Receipts are required for all expenses over $25. Reimbursement is typically processed within 10 business days.
