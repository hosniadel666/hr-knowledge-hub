TechVista Solutions — Benefits FAQ

Health Insurance

Q: What health insurance plans does TechVista offer?
A: TechVista offers two health insurance plans:
- PPO Plan: Lower deductible ($500 individual / $1,000 family), wider network, higher monthly premium. Best for employees who visit doctors frequently or have ongoing medical needs.
- HDHP Plan: Higher deductible ($1,500 individual / $3,000 family), lower monthly premium, HSA-eligible. Best for generally healthy employees who want to save on monthly costs and build tax-advantaged savings.

Q: How much does the company contribute to health insurance?
A: TechVista covers 80% of the monthly premium for individual coverage and 70% for family coverage on both plans. The employee is responsible for the remaining portion, deducted pre-tax from each paycheck.

Q: When does health insurance coverage begin?
A: Coverage begins on the first day of the month following your start date. If you start on the first of the month, coverage begins immediately.

Dental and Vision

Q: Is dental and vision insurance included?
A: Yes. TechVista provides dental and vision coverage at no additional cost to employees for individual plans. Family dental and vision coverage is available at a subsidized rate of $45/month. Dental coverage includes two cleanings per year, basic and major restorative procedures with varying copays. Vision coverage includes an annual eye exam and $200 allowance for frames or contacts.

Retirement Benefits

Q: Does TechVista offer a 401(k) plan?
A: Yes. TechVista offers a 401(k) plan with a company match of 100% on the first 4% of your salary that you contribute. For example, if you earn $100,000 and contribute 4% ($4,000), TechVista will match the full $4,000.

Q: What is the vesting schedule for the 401(k) match?
A: The company match follows a 3-year graded vesting schedule:
- After 1 year: 33% vested
- After 2 years: 66% vested
- After 3 years: 100% vested
Your own contributions are always 100% vested immediately.

Q: When can I enroll in the 401(k)?
A: You are eligible to enroll in the 401(k) plan immediately upon hire. Enrollment can be completed through our benefits portal.

Health Savings Account (HSA) and Flexible Spending Account (FSA)

Q: What is the difference between an HSA and an FSA?
A: An HSA is available only to employees enrolled in the HDHP plan. Contributions are tax-deductible, the funds roll over year to year, and TechVista contributes $500 annually to your HSA. An FSA is available to employees on either plan. You can contribute up to $3,050 per year pre-tax, but funds must be used by March 15 of the following year (use-it-or-lose-it, with a $610 grace amount).

Life Insurance and Disability

Q: Does TechVista provide life insurance?
A: Yes. TechVista provides basic life insurance at 2x your annual salary at no cost to you, up to a maximum of $500,000. Supplemental life insurance is available for purchase at group rates for you, your spouse, and dependents.

Q: What about disability coverage?
A: TechVista provides both short-term and long-term disability insurance at no cost:
- Short-term disability: 60% of salary for up to 26 weeks after a 14-day waiting period
- Long-term disability: 60% of salary after short-term disability is exhausted, until age 65 or return to work

Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

Q: What is the Employee Assistance Program?
A: The EAP provides confidential counseling and support services at no cost to employees and their household members. Services include up to 8 counseling sessions per issue per year, financial planning assistance, legal consultation, and work-life balance resources. Call 1-800-555-0199 or visit our EAP portal for access.

Tuition Reimbursement

Q: Does TechVista offer tuition reimbursement?
A: Yes. Full-time employees who have completed 6 months of service are eligible for up to $5,250 per calendar year in tuition reimbursement. Eligible expenses include tuition and required textbooks for job-related courses at accredited institutions. A grade of B or higher (or equivalent) is required for reimbursement. Approval from your manager and HR is required before enrollment.

Additional Benefits

Q: What other benefits does TechVista offer?
A: Additional benefits include:
- Gym membership: 50% discount on memberships at participating fitness centers (up to $50/month reimbursement)
- Commuter benefits: Pre-tax transit and parking benefits up to IRS limits ($300/month for transit, $300/month for parking)
- Employee referral bonus: $5,000 for successful engineering referrals, $3,000 for other positions
- Professional development: $1,500 annual budget for conferences, courses, and certifications
- Home office stipend: $500 one-time setup allowance for remote work equipment

Open Enrollment

Q: When is open enrollment?
A: Open enrollment occurs annually in November, with changes taking effect January 1. Outside of open enrollment, you may only change benefits during a qualifying life event (marriage, birth of a child, loss of other coverage, etc.). Qualifying life event changes must be made within 30 days of the event.
